# Audit patterns

## Claim ledger

Classify material statements as fact, inference, value, promise, testimonial, authority, or urgency. Mark evidence `verified`, `sourced`, `unsupported`, `contradicted`, or `unknown`. Unsupported does not prove intentional lying.

## Deception patterns

Fabricated fact; material omission; false authority; false consensus; false scarcity; certainty inflation; identity-flattering trap; enemy manufacture; causal simplification; testimonial laundering; promise laundering; truth-indifferent optimization.

## Hard red flags

Fabricated evidence; invented countdown or popularity; hidden price, renewal, risk or cancellation; public shaming; compulsory confession; retaliation for leaving; manufactured enemies; vulnerability exploitation; irreversible first action; missing practical exit; concealed persuasive intent.

## Assessment

- `acceptable`: supported material claims, visible intent and terms, alternatives and exit.
- `caution`: material pressure, incomplete evidence, or asymmetric friction.
- `manipulative`: concealed pressure, enclosure, or lock-in narrows informed choice.
- `deceptive`: material falsehood, false attribution, false urgency, or deliberate concealment is evidenced.
- `insufficient evidence`: context is too incomplete.

## Rewrite

Replace asserted audience diagnosis with a recognizable scene; vague authority with a checkable source; certainty with conditions; false urgency with the real window; identity threat with dignity and alternatives; grand salvation with a credible path and reversible action; compulsory ritual with voluntary cadence and exit. Add correction, complaint, cancellation, and harm monitoring.
