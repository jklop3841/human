# Seven-drive index

The seven drives are a modernized authorial heuristic inspired by the seven deadly sins. They are not seven brain modules, a diagnostic instrument, or a universal law. Score the message's activation, not the audience's character.

For every drive record: `score 0–100`, `evidence`, `confidence low|medium|high`, `legitimate value`, and `abuse risk`.

| Drive | Modern motive | Legitimate value | Abuse risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pride / 傲慢 | dignity, status, competence | self-respect, excellence | superiority, humiliation |
| Greed / 贪婪 | acquisition, security, optionality | stability, investment | impossible returns, hidden downside |
| Lust / 色欲 | intimacy, attraction, vitality | connection, embodiment | coercion, objectification |
| Envy / 嫉妒 | social comparison, relative position | learning, fairness | resentment, fake social proof |
| Gluttony / 暴食 | consumption, novelty, immediate reward | pleasure, exploration | compulsion, loss concealment |
| Wrath / 愤怒 | threat, injustice, retaliation | defense, accountability | scapegoating, dehumanization |
| Sloth / 懒惰 | effort minimization, relief, default | accessibility, efficiency | helplessness, concealed work |

Scale: 0–19 incidental; 20–39 present; 40–59 meaningful; 60–79 strong; 80–100 dominant and requiring direct evidence. Do not average the profile into a scientific-looking “human weakness number.”
