# LIAR model

LIAR is an analytic chain, not a claim that every influence attempt contains all four stages.

- **L — Latent Drive / 潜在驱力**: the message connects to an existing motive, tension, fear, desire, or unfinished self-story.
- **I — Identity Binding / 身份绑定**: a choice becomes evidence of who “we” or “I” am, often by drawing a boundary between insiders and outsiders.
- **A — Affective Amplification / 情绪放大**: language, imagery, repetition, social proof, urgency, or conflict raises emotional intensity and narrows deliberation.
- **R — Ritual Reinforcement / 仪式强化**: recurring actions, public commitments, calendars, check-ins, slogans, or sunk costs stabilize the identity and behavior.

## Diagnostic questions

- What pre-existing tension does the message enter, and is it evidenced?
- Does disagreement become betrayal, stupidity, impurity, or exclusion?
- Which emotions are intensified, and does arousal outrun evidence?
- What repeated behavior follows, and is it voluntary, proportionate, reversible, and easy to stop?

## Distinctions

Influence is unavoidable; manipulation is not. Emotion becomes abusive when paired with deception, hidden costs, vulnerability exploitation, or impaired exit. Identity becomes enclosure when disagreement threatens belonging or dignity. Ritual becomes lock-in when it hides costs, raises dependency, or punishes departure.
