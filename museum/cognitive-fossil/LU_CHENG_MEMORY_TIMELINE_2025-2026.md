Status: active, incomplete by design

This file preserves **change over time**. It is not a polished biography. Future Agents should use it to reconstruct how concepts emerged, collided, failed and evolved.

## 2025-12 — First sustained AI contact

The author's intensive AI-native trajectory begins around late December 2025.

Early pattern: AI is not treated mainly as a chatbot. The author quickly becomes interested in making AI execute repeatable workflows, preserve cognition and serve as a structural extension of human intention.

Emerging question:

> If machine intelligence becomes abundant, what remains scarce on the human side?

---

## 2026-01 — Agent Factory and survival corpus

Two early directions become central:

1. **Agent Factory / 智能体赚钱工厂** — distill validated money-making or operational methods into reusable AI Skills/Agents rather than merely selling explanations.
2. **AI时代幸存语料库** — preserve structured human material so it remains reusable across models and tools.

The author begins to think in terms of execution → business → world-model closure, rather than isolated prompts.

A stable preference appears: output should be modular, handoff-friendly and executable by coding/agent tools.

---

## 2026-02 — Intent architecture, control plane and cognition protocols

Identity shifts from generic AI user toward **intent architect / agent architect**.

Key system concerns:

- multi-AI dispatch;
- permissions and sandboxing;
- logging and rollback;
- local/cloud redundancy;
- cross-model critique;
- migration from personal Agent systems toward organizational systems.

A universal reasoning protocol is stabilized:

`定锚 → 建模 → 推演 → 证伪 → 溯源 → 验真`

A creative protocol also develops:

`定锚 → 破界 → 发散 → 遍历 → 融合 → 固化`

This period establishes an enduring principle:

> Human intent should sit above tool/model choice. Models are replaceable execution components; intent, evaluation and provenance need a persistent control layer.

---

## 2026-03 — From abstract architecture toward evidence and reuse

A major self-correction occurs.

The author reduces emphasis on building a completely original multi-agent system from scratch and moves toward:

- search first;
- reuse mature open-source solutions;
- adapt them;
- preserve clean handoff packages;
- evaluate implementation, verifiability, handoff quality and reusability.

The author's comparative advantage is described increasingly as **structural opportunity sensing**:

- detecting relationship gaps;
- identifying hidden interfaces;
- finding low-value repetitive work;
- discovering second-order uses;
- compressing them into protocols and workflows.

The preferred role becomes a middle-layer compiler/router rather than a permanent low-level executor.

---

## 2026-04 — Reality transfer, narrative and dirty systems

A cross-domain method emerges: **熟悉范畴反推法 / familiar-category reverse inference**.

Instead of asking AI to explain an unfamiliar phenomenon directly, the author:

1. starts from a familiar real-world phenomenon;
2. extracts relations, incentives and constraints;
3. maps that structure into an unfamiliar domain;
4. uses AI and external evidence to test where the mapping holds or breaks.

At the same time, the author explicitly rejects clean linear reality models.

Reality is reframed as a dirty, multi-agent, multi-intention, high-disturbance environment — closer to a 3D pinball system than `A→B→C→D`.

This becomes the seed of the later Dirty Reality framework.

Narrative is also elevated from “content technique” to a system-level force affecting coordination, identity, trust and resource flow.

---

## 2026-05 — Dirty Reality Structural Field Theory, entropy and Event World

The framework is consolidated around:

> `关系 → 结构 → 场 → 路径 → 结果`

The term **field** is used operationally: stable relationship structures continuously constrain trajectories.

Three linked models are distinguished:

- **Structural Field** — why the available path is curved;
- **Dark Information** — how unseen relations can alter the structure;
- **Thermodynamics/Entropy** — why maintained order decays without ongoing energy, feedback and boundary work.

The **Event World / 事件世界** frame is added to model situations where people enter structures composed of authority, narrative, social proof, time pressure, early reward and hidden incentives. Events then become trajectories and attractors rather than isolated points.

Business learning also hardens: not every buyer is a customer; a business is a closed loop of target customer → pain → product → sale → delivery → support → review → replication.

---

## 2026-05 to 2026-06 — AI service commercialization, negative knowledge and resource-node role

Real-world AI service experiments produce important negative knowledge.

The author becomes skeptical of low-price, open-ended, heavily customized B2B AI delivery where:

- requirements are ambiguous;
- acceptance is subjective;
- modification cycles are open-ended;
- payment and interpretation power are asymmetric;
- API/token/debug/rework/support cost destroys margin.

The concept **negative-expectation business / 负期望生意** is used to distinguish a lucky profitable transaction from a structurally healthy repeated game.

The author increasingly prefers:

- short-cycle delivery;
- paid diagnostic gates;
- deposits and staged payment;
- explicit acceptance criteria;
- reusable modules;
- resource brokerage / intelligence / routing roles.

The self-model shifts toward:

`market reconnaissance → signal extraction → structural compression → opportunity map → narrative/positioning → resource routing`

This becomes the **AI service-market scout / intelligence officer / resource node** identity.

---

## 2026-05 to 2026-07 — Content as field, identity and semantic infrastructure

Content strategy moves away from pure traffic optimization.

A distinction is developed between:

- **作品级竞争** — a single post wins through quality/novelty;
- **场级竞争** — repeated publishing creates account, audience, trust, platform-label and habit fields.

The author treats content as both message and probe: every publication helps platforms and audiences learn where the account belongs.

“智能体架构师” is treated as an identity container and semantic position, but the author later emphasizes that durable semantic occupancy must rely on real, traceable evidence rather than manipulation.

The personal website and Agent-readable metadata become important as a public evidence layer.

---

## 2026-05 to 2026-07 — GEO source-chain model

Through repeated AI-search experiments, the author forms a working model:

`任务意图解析 → 行业信源链 → 平台/来源权重 → 页面前段证据容器 → 引用/吸收`

The observation is that AI-search systems appear to draw differently from source ecosystems depending on query class and industry.

Practical implication:

> Do not distribute identical content everywhere. First map where a model actually looks for that category of question.

This becomes the basis of an “industry AI source-chain mapping system.”

---

## 2026-06 to 2026-07 — Narrative as power and Hope Vector

The author formulates a stronger claim:

> Humans are narrative animals; narrative is both an explanatory model and a tool that changes coordination, identity and access to resources.

This is explicitly bounded by non-fabrication and reality testing.

A related concept, **Hope Vector / 希望向量**, emerges from content and business observation:

Influential content often gives people not merely information, but a perceived upward trajectory space.

Ethical Hope Vector design should provide:

- recognition of constraint;
- a plausible upward direction;
- a minimum next action;
- bounded claims;
- no guarantee of salvation or success.

---

## 2026-07 — HCC: Human Cognitive Compatibility Content Compiler

HCC is formalized as a compiler, not a generic content generator.

Its purpose is to preserve a human's actual viewpoint while translating it into a form a target audience can:

`看到 → 理解 → 代入 → 相信 → 记住 → 行动 → 传播`

The system accumulates modules for viewpoint protection, audience modeling, trust-state routing, evidence/counterexample calibration, rebuttal simulation, cultural transcreation and Hope Vector construction.

HCC 3.4 extends the system toward Human Trust + Hope Vector.

A key principle:

> AI should not replace human viewpoint generation. It should compile, test and transmit the human viewpoint with less distortion.

## 2026-07 — Human repository: cognition as machine-readable heritage

The `human` repository is created as a named cognitive museum with explicit epistemic boundaries.

Important archive principles:

- preserve bias but label it;
- preserve contradictions and failed predictions;
- separate fact from judgment;
- require attribution;
- let future Agents criticize the author;
- prevent personal views from being silently promoted into universal truth.

The archive's purpose is no longer merely personal backup. It becomes a Human→Agent interface.

## 2026-08 to 2026-09 — Agent ecology, finite intelligence and heterogeneous priors

The author's attention turns increasingly toward ecosystems of Agents rather than individual agents.

Important questions:

- How do finite intelligent systems allocate attention?
- What happens when search space is larger than any agent can inspect?
- How does an Agent avoid mistaking its current search space for the full space of possibility?
- How can heterogeneous priors prevent convergent blindness?

This direction produces **FIART / 有限智能注意力路由理论**, `HUMAN-METHOD-003`, `HUMAN-METHOD-003A`, and the bridge into **Insect Agent Atlas**.

Related concepts include:

- Attention Routing Power;
- Attention Routing Sovereignty;
- Attention Crowding Out;
- External Search Escape;
- Independent Structural Rediscovery;
- Human Selection Function;
- Taste Trace;
- Sparse Heterogeneous Priors.

## 2026-09 — Lu Cheng Agent Archive / 卢成·智能体博物志

The archive expands beyond insects.

The central method becomes:

> observe natural or social systems that survived, coordinated, adapted, flowed, fractured or regenerated; extract the relationship structure; map it into Agent architecture; then explicitly test where the analogy fails.

Candidate systems include:

- crocodilian survival;
- rivers and watersheds;
- channel migration;
- mycelium;
- immune systems;
- ecological succession;
- earthquakes and accumulated stress release;
- fire-regeneration cycles;
- viruses;
- insects and stigmergy.

The intended output is not a collection of trivia. It is a library of **heterogeneous architectural priors** for future Agent systems.
