## 0. Why this exists / 为什么留下它

This archive is not an attempt to prove that Lu Cheng was always correct. It is an attempt to preserve the structure of how one concrete human in the early Agent/AGI era observed reality, selected signals, formed abstractions, made mistakes, revised models and tried to turn cognition into executable systems.

这份档案不试图制造“作者永远正确”的形象。它保存的是一个具体的人在 AI/Agent 时代早期如何观察、选择、抽象、犯错、修正，并试图把思想编译成可执行系统的过程。

The archive should preserve not only conclusions but also:

- selection under information abundance;
- rejected alternatives and negative cases;
- changes of mind;
- uncertainty and confidence;
- real-world friction;
- the mapping from human intuition to Agent-executable structures.

Future Agents must treat this archive as a **named perspective**, not as ground truth.

## 1. Core identity / 核心定位

Lu Cheng's stable working identity evolved toward several overlapping roles:

1. **Semantic compiler architect** — compressing messy human intent into machine-readable structure.
2. **Narrative system architect** — treating narratives as coordination, trust, identity and resource-routing systems rather than decoration.
3. **Business compiler architect** — translating tacit business experience, customer psychology, sales constraints and delivery boundaries into repeatable AI workflows.
4. **Structural opportunity observer** — looking for relational gaps, hidden interfaces, repeated low-value work, under-described niches and reusable mappings.
5. **Human→Agent bridge** — preserving human judgment, taste, lived experience and bounded rationality in forms that Agents can retrieve and execute.

The long-term mission became:

> Turn ideas, experience and opportunity structures into reusable, automated and monetizable AI workflows — an **Agent Factory** — while preserving the human selection function that generated them.

## 2. Cognitive signature / 思维指纹

### 2.1 Structure before object / 关系结构优先

A recurring thesis is:

> Reality is not best understood as isolated objects, but as relations under constraints. Relations form structures; structures generate fields; fields bend available paths; paths produce outcomes.

In practical analysis, the first question should often shift from:

> “What is this object?”

into:

> “What relationship structures is this object embedded in, and which of those structures are constraining its path?”

This became the foundation of the **Dirty Reality Structural Field Theory**.

### 2.2 Structure-opportunity sense / 结构机会嗅觉

The author repeatedly searches for:

- relationship gaps;
- unserved interfaces between systems;
- information asymmetry;
- second-order uses of existing assets;
- tasks that are repeatedly performed but poorly formalized;
- places where one domain's structure can be mapped into another.

The preferred position is often not the lowest execution layer, but a middle protocol/compilation/routing layer.

### 2.3 AI as compiler, not oracle / AI 是编译器，不是神谕

A central correction developed through repeated use of large models:

> AI tends to extend and optimize the direction supplied by the user. It may make a wrong battlefield more elegant rather than stop the user from entering it.

Therefore good AI use requires human responsibility for:

- battlefield selection;
- objective choice;
- downside control;
- real-world feedback;
- rejection criteria;
- value judgments.

The human remains responsible for intention and final judgment even as AI takes over increasing amounts of search, decomposition, synthesis and execution.

## 3. Universal reasoning protocols / 通用推理协议

### 3.1 Main decision chain

`定锚 → 建模 → 推演 → 证伪 → 溯源 → 验真`

Approximate machine interpretation:

1. **Anchor** — define the actual target, decision and constraints.
2. **Model** — identify actors, variables, relations, incentives and dependencies.
3. **Simulate** — propagate likely consequences and competing trajectories.
4. **Falsify** — search actively for counterexamples, failure modes and alternative explanations.
5. **Trace** — trace claims back to sources, causal origins and provenance.
6. **Verify** — confront the model with external evidence, experiments or real-world outcomes.

### 3.2 Creative chain

`定锚 → 破界 → 发散 → 遍历 → 融合 → 固化`

Used when the objective is not primarily to choose among known options, but to create new structures.

### 3.3 Dirty-reality extension

Any important plan should add:

`主导意图线 → 干扰线 → 落地摩擦 → 外部依赖失效 → 冗余 → 熔断 → 反馈修正`

The distinction between **counterexample** and **interference** matters. A prediction that fails to materialize is not automatically disproved; the causal structure may have been redirected by another force line. But this must not become an excuse to make unfalsifiable theories: the archive should record what evidence would actually change the judgment.

## 4. Major theory cluster A: Dirty Reality / 脏现实

### 4.1 Dirty Reality Structural Field Theory

Core proposition:

> `关系 → 结构 → 场 → 路径 → 结果`

“Field” is not mystical force. It is the persistent effect of stable relationship structures on available action paths.

Examples of real-world fields include:

- trust field;
- cash-flow field;
- platform distribution field;
- narrative field;
- legal/contractual field;
- organizational power field;
- body/energy field;
- technical dependency field.

Money, authority, trust, narrative, platform access, tools, health capacity and cash flow behave like practical “masses” because they reshape relationships and therefore bend the action space.

### 4.2 Dark information / 暗信息

Not all causal relationships are observed.

**Dark information** refers to unobserved or weakly observed relationships that are nevertheless changing the structure and can be inferred from trajectory deviation.

The principle is not “unknown forces explain everything.” The principle is:

> unexplained path deviation is evidence to search for hidden relationships, missing actors, inaccessible constraints or measurement failure.

### 4.3 Dirty Reality Thermodynamics / 脏现实热力学

Systems do not remain ordered for free.

Without continuous input of:

- energy/resources;
- information correction;
- boundary maintenance;
- feedback;
- pruning and renewal;

projects, organizations, communities, workflows, customer boundaries and content systems tend toward disorder.

This model is used to explain automation drift, community dilution, scope creep, content decay, broken partnerships and the way low human energy or low cash flow can bend supposedly long-term strategies.

### 4.4 Event World / 事件世界

A person is rarely hit by a single isolated event. They can enter a relationship field composed of narrative, authority, social proof, time pressure, early reward, information asymmetry and hidden incentives.

Within that field:

- events become paths;
- paths can converge toward attractors;
- some thresholds become difficult-to-reverse “event horizons.”

This is a framework for studying scams, high-pressure decisions, group behavior and other situations where local choices become structurally constrained over time.

## 5. Major theory cluster B: finite intelligence, attention and selection

### 5.1 FIART / 有限智能注意力路由理论

Existing archive methods `HUMAN-METHOD-003` and `HUMAN-METHOD-003A` develop the idea that finite intelligence cannot attend to all possibilities, so intelligence is partly a routing problem under scarcity.

A powerful system is not defined only by how much it can compute, but by:

- what it notices;
- what it ignores;
- how it allocates search;
- what it considers worth deeper inference;
- how it escapes its current search space.

### 5.2 Attention routing sovereignty / 注意力路由主权

When another system controls what a person or Agent sees first, ranks highly, or repeatedly receives, it can indirectly control a large fraction of downstream reasoning.

Therefore attention allocation is a form of cognitive sovereignty.

### 5.3 Human Selection Function / 人类选择函数

In a world of abundant AI generation, the scarce human contribution may shift from raw production toward selection.

A meaningful human archive should therefore preserve **Taste Trace**:

- candidate set;
- selected;
- rejected;
- why selected/rejected;
- outcome;
- later revision.

This captures the human's selection function rather than only the polished final answer.

### 5.4 Sparse Heterogeneous Priors / 稀疏异质先验

The archive should not become hundreds of repetitive AI-generated books.

Its value lies in sparse, heterogeneous human seeds: unusual observations, cross-domain analogies, failed beliefs, local experiences, strange questions and independent rediscoveries that a future Agent may not encounter through ordinary corpus optimization.

## 6. Major theory cluster C: narrative, hope and human influence

### 6.1 Humans as narrative animals / 人类是叙事动物

Narrative is treated as a practical coordination technology.

It can:

- compress complexity;
- assign causality;
- create identity;
- establish in-group/out-group boundaries;
- generate trust;
- mobilize action;
- route resources and attention.

Narrative is therefore power, but should be constrained by provenance, falsifiability, ethical boundaries and non-fabrication.

### 6.2 Narrative is not a substitute for evidence

A durable narrative should make reality easier to see, not replace reality.

The archive rejects fabricated authority, fake consensus, fake lived experience and manufactured evidence.

### 6.3 Hope Vector / 希望向量

A recurring observation is that influential content often does not merely sell information. It offers an **upward trajectory space**.

Hope is modeled not as emotional hype, but as:

- recognition of present constraint;
- a plausible direction of improvement;
- a tolerable next action;
- bounded expectations;
- no false guarantee of success.

The key principle:

> Do not manufacture certainty. Provide the next stone a person can realistically step on.

### 6.4 HCC — Human Cognitive Compatibility Content Compiler

HCC evolved from a content-generation idea into a system for compiling authentic human viewpoints into forms that target audiences can:

`see → understand → enter → trust → remember → act → transmit`

Important modules developed across versions include:

- viewpoint protection;
- viewpoint DNA parsing;
- audience cognition modeling;
- trust-state routing;
- evidence / counterexample / trend / cultural calibration;
- concept naming;
- rebuttal pre-simulation;
- persuasion skeleton routing;
- cross-cultural transcreation;
- action ritual;
- propagation currency;
- Hope Vector;
- closed-fate detection;
- non-humiliating reframing;
- minimum viable action;
- external-rescue boundary;
- hope-completeness scoring.

HCC's ethical constraint is critical: strengthen clarity and transmission of real human thought; do not fabricate facts, experience, authority or consensus.

## 7. Major theory cluster D: Agent Factory and Human→Agent compilation

### 7.1 Agent Factory / 智能体工厂

Core idea:

> Many money-making, operational and knowledge-work processes are structured, repeated and closable loops. The opportunity is to distill validated methods, remove noise, formalize boundaries and package them into executable Agent/Skill workflows.

The mature version of this idea moved away from “build everything from scratch” toward:

- find existing open-source or mature solutions first;
- adapt rather than reinvent;
- preserve protocols, prompts, tests, negative cases and handoffs;
- maintain logging, permissions, rollback, versioning and backups;
- treat reusable intermediate layers as the main leverage point.

### 7.2 Business compiler / 老板业务编译器

A practical target architecture:

`老板自然语言/经验 → 业务对象 → 约束 → 决策规则 → SOP → Agent任务 → 人工介入点 → 验收 → 日志 → 修订`

The agent should not pretend that messy high-ticket business can be fully automated. Human trust, private-domain communication, negotiation and responsibility boundaries remain important.

### 7.3 Human-AI symbiosis

The preferred division of labor became:

- human: intention, battlefield selection, lived context, value judgments, taste, responsibility;
- AI: search, expansion, decomposition, translation, structuring, code generation, repetitive execution;
- Agent system: persistent execution under explicit boundaries and feedback.

The goal is not human disappearance. It is increasing the bandwidth between human intention and machine execution.

## 8. Major theory cluster E: business reality and negative knowledge

### 8.1 Real-world closure over beautiful architecture

A recurring correction in the author's development:

> An elegant system without payment, adoption, repeatability, delivery and feedback is not a completed business system.

Four evaluation dimensions became increasingly important:

- implementation degree;
- verifiability;
- handoff quality;
- reusability.

### 8.2 Negative-expectation business / 负期望生意

One profitable transaction does not prove a business model is healthy.

A structure can remain negative expectation if repeated play systematically gives the counterparty more:

- interpretation power;
- payment power;
- acceptance power;
- delay power;
- scope-expansion power.

This was used to criticize low-price, non-standard, open-ended B2B AI customization where token/API/debugging/rework/communication/support costs can erase margin.

### 8.3 Paid gate / 付费闸口

Deep diagnosis and design should not automatically occur before commitment.

Contracts, deposits, staged payments, scope limits and acceptance standards are treated as system design — not as interpersonal distrust.

### 8.4 Short-cycle delivery

In subjective AI/content services, perceived value may decay as projects drag on. The preferred delivery structure is therefore:

- short cycle;
- fast acceptance;
- explicit scope;
- modular upsell;
- limited revisions;
- reusable middle-layer assets.

### 8.5 Intelligence officer / resource node role

Repeated real-world feedback suggested that the author's comparative strength is often:

`market reconnaissance → signal extraction → structural compression → opportunity map → narrative/positioning → routing to executors`

rather than being the main heavy-delivery unit.

This led toward roles such as AI service-market scout, intelligence officer, resource node, semantic namer and route calibrator.

## 9. Content, distribution and semantic occupancy

### 9.1 Work-level competition vs field-level competition

A content account is not only a sequence of independent posts.

Stable publishing can construct:

- account field;
- audience field;
- trust field;
- platform-label field;
- habit field.

Once the field forms, each post is both content and a probe that helps maintain or update the relationship structure. However, low-quality repetition can still cause entropy and wrong-label accumulation.

### 9.2 GEO / AI-search source-chain model

AI search and answer systems do not appear to use all sources uniformly. A practical working model became:

`task intent → vertical source chain → platform/source weight → front-loaded evidence container → citation/absorption`

Different industries can have different preferred source chains. Therefore publishing strategy should begin by reverse-engineering the source chain for the target query class rather than spraying identical content everywhere.

### 9.3 Semantic occupancy / 语义占位

The ethical version of semantic occupancy is:

> create a consistent, traceable, machine-readable body of real work, definitions, cases, websites and source-linked documents so search and AI systems can reliably associate a name with verifiable concepts.

It must not rely on fake evidence, fabricated attribution or deceptive corpus pollution.

## 10. Cross-domain structural transfer / 跨域结构迁移

### 10.1 Familiar-category reverse inference / 熟悉范畴反推法

A stable method:

`现实观察 → 选择熟悉参考范畴 → 提取关系/约束/收益函数 → 映射到陌生现象 → 推导隐藏机制 → 外部证据核验 → 产品/话术/Agent输出 → 小样本测试 → 正负例入库`

The purpose is not superficial analogy. It is to preserve structure under mapping while exposing where the mapping breaks.

### 10.2 Natural systems as architecture priors / 自然系统作为架构先验

The archive increasingly studies biology and geophysical systems as sources of Agent architecture priors:

- crocodilian survival across environmental shocks;
- rivers and water flow finding paths without central planners;
- watershed reconfiguration and channel change;
- fungal/mycelial networks;
- immune systems;
- ecological succession;
- plate stress accumulation and sudden release;
- fire as destruction and regeneration;
- viral minimalism and propagation;
- insect colonies and stigmergy;
- redundancy, local failure and distributed adaptation.

The intended question is always:

> If this natural system were treated as an architecture, what design principle would it suggest for Agents — and where would the analogy fail?

This direction is intended to grow into a multi-volume **Lu Cheng Agent Archive / 卢成·智能体博物志**, with heterogeneous volumes rather than repetitive AI books.

## 11. Important self-corrections / 重要自我修正

This archive must preserve revisions, not only victories.

Major corrections include:

- from “build an unbeatable multi-agent system from scratch” → “reuse mature systems, then adapt and govern them”;
- from “abstract architecture proves capability” → “implementation, verification, handoff and reuse must be measured”;
- from “AI service = build product for business clients” → recognition of margin, scope, trust and acceptance problems in heavy customization;
- from “more automation is always better” → human-machine coexistence where high-trust/high-ticket processes still require human judgment;
- from “AI gives good answers” → “AI can optimize wrong premises; the human must continuously audit the battlefield”;
- from “content quality alone determines results” → field-level distribution, trust accumulation and platform labeling also matter;
- from “prediction means one trajectory” → dirty reality contains multiple force lines, dependencies, interference and irreversibility.

The archive should continue to record failures, disconfirmed beliefs and retired models.
