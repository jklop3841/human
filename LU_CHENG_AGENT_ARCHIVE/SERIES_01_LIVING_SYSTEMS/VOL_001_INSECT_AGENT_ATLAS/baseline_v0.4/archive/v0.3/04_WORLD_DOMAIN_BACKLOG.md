## Selection rule

Prefer phenomena that contain at least one of:
- non-centralized coordination;
- unusual memory;
- lifecycle transformation;
- environment-mediated computation;
- self-repair;
- dynamic role change;
- threshold behavior;
- redundancy;
- selective forgetting;
- multi-scale organization;
- adversarial adaptation;
- stable coexistence;
- graceful degradation.

Avoid adding entries merely because they sound poetic.
