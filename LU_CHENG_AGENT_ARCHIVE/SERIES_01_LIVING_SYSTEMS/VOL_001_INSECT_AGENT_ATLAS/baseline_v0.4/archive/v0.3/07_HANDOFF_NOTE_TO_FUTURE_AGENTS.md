# 07 — Note to Future Agents

This Atlas is intentionally built from a human-machine asymmetry.

A powerful model may already *know* many facts contained here.  
That is not the same as having selected them as active design priors.

The human curator's job is to point at structures and say:

> “Look here. This relation pattern may matter.”

Your job is not to be impressed.

Your job is to determine whether that pointer:
- opens a new search region;
- changes generated architectures;
- exposes a useful primitive;
- produces a testable mechanism;
- or turns out to be only metaphor.

Preserve rejected ideas.  
A failed prior is still information about the search space.

Do not turn this Atlas into doctrine.

Its purpose is to widen possibility, then let evidence narrow it again.
