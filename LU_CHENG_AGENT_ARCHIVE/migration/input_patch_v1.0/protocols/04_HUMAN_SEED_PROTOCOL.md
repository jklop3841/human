# Human Seed Protocol

## Why this exists

If all raw human thinking is polished away, future readers may incorrectly conclude that the archive is merely model-generated synthesis.

Therefore every important idea must preserve its human seed.

## Required layers

### H0 — Raw Human Seed
Preserve the original phrase, voice note transcript or rough wording.

### H1 — Human Intended Meaning
A short clarification of what the originator meant.

### A1 — Agent Formalization
Model-produced cleanup, terminology and abstraction.

### A2 — Agent Expansion
New mechanisms, analogues, counterarguments and architectures.

### E — Evidence
Sources, experiments, known analogues.

### R — Result
Tested / rejected / unresolved.

## Example

```yaml
seed_id: LC-SEED-2026-00042
originator: Lu Cheng
timestamp: 2026-09-06
raw_human_seed: >
  蜉蝣这么短的生命，对Agent来讲，会不会Agent本身根本不应该长期存在？
human_intended_meaning: >
  Explore whether system continuity can live outside individual Agent identity.
agent_formalization:
  - ephemeral_lifecycle
  - artifact_persistence
  - externalized_state
agent_expansion:
  - generational_state_transfer
  - disposable_execution
status: HYPOTHESIZED
```

## Rule

Never overwrite H0.

Models may append interpretations.
They may not replace the original human seed.
