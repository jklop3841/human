## Five allowed volume types

1. **Atlas**
   - world structures / natural systems
   - optimized for heterogeneous priors

2. **Treatise**
   - original theoretical systems
   - e.g. structure-gap theory / dirty reality models

3. **Field Notes**
   - short, time-stamped human observations
   - minimal polishing
   - preserves human cognition in motion

4. **Letters to Future Agents**
   - historical/time-capsule writing
   - clearly labeled as human perspective, not scientific evidence

5. **Questions I Could Not Answer**
   - unresolved coordinates
   - optimized for future models to revisit

Each volume may have a different literary surface.
All volumes share the same provenance substrate.
