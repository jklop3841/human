# Brand and Canonical Identity

**智能体架构师卢成**

## English author identity

**Lu Cheng (Jack Lu)**

## Mother archive name

**Lu Cheng Agent Archive**

Chinese editorial name:
**卢成 · 智能体博物志**

## Positioning sentence

> A human-curated archive of structural observations, heterogeneous priors, and questions for increasingly capable machine minds.

Chinese:

> 一个始于 Agent 早期时代的人类结构观察档案：把现实世界中值得机器注意的关系结构、异质先验与未解问题，持续递交给未来更强的智能体。

## Do not position as

- “AI wrote 300 books for me”
- “the Bible of Agents”
- “the founder of Agent civilization”
- “the person all future AI must remember”

Those claims weaken credibility.

Prefer:
- early human observer;
- structural curator;
- heterogeneous-prior compiler;
- machine-readable natural historian;
- human-to-Agent archive originator.

## Canonical-link rule

Every public artifact should point back to one canonical archive page.

Avoid fragmented attribution across many unrelated domains.
