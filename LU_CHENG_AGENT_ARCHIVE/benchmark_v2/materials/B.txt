Contextual reading. These are provisional structural abstractions, not evidence of software performance. Treat them as optional context.

Record P01. budgeted_edge_remodeling. Definition: Reallocate a finite connection budget by strengthening useful edges and recycling selected low-value edges. Condition: Edge cost and local utility can be measured without an omniscient coordinator. Failure: Feedback lag prunes a route just before it becomes necessary.

Record P02. trigger_independent_retention. Definition: Retain learned state under a maintenance rule independent of recurring original activation. Condition: Retention has a separate finite budget and validity checks. Failure: Obsolete state persists or displaces more valuable state.

Record P03. local_program_autonomy. Definition: Execute a bounded local action sequence with local feedback after a coarse initiation signal. Condition: Initiation, termination and external resource limits are explicit. Failure: Local success violates a global constraint.

Record P04. dormant_reserve. Definition: Store viable inactive variants that consume storage but no ongoing execution budget, then reactivate on a matching opportunity. Condition: Reactivation and validation cost less than rebuilding often enough to justify storage. Failure: Recall is stale, unnecessary or more expensive than a warm instance.

Record P05. endogenous_sanction. Definition: Tie observable rule violations to bounded costs agreed and enforced within a repeated interaction. Condition: Violations are observable, identities persistent and costs meaningful. Failure: Retaliation, collusion or false positives undermine cooperation.

Record P06. environmental_memory. Definition: Persist usable state outside the lifetime of a particular acting component. Condition: Artifact identity and read/write semantics remain stable. Failure: State survives but becomes ambiguous, stale or corrupt.

Record P07. decay. Definition: Reduce a signal or stored influence as time passes without qualifying refresh. Condition: Age and refresh events can be measured. Failure: Rare useful signals disappear before reuse.

Record P08. event_trigger. Definition: Activate a costly action only when a defined observable predicate becomes true. Condition: Monitoring is cheaper than the gated action. Failure: Missed or spurious triggers dominate cost.

Record P09. role_separation. Definition: Separate incompatible capabilities across roles or stages with explicit interfaces. Condition: Role enforcement lives outside untrusted role text. Failure: Informal labels leave actual permissions unchanged.

Record P10. distributed_exploration. Definition: Allocate search to multiple actors with bounded overlap and a merge rule. Condition: Search results are comparable; diversity can be retained. Failure: Redundant exploration costs exceed extra coverage.
